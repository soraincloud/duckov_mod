## English Description

Version: v1.0.0
Updated: 2026-03-09
Update Notes: Initial release.

After entering a level, the mod automatically starts a 10-second countdown. When the countdown ends, it attempts to deduct `$168` from the player. If the combined account balance and carried cash are insufficient, no money is deducted and a notice is shown.

### Important Notice

- This mod may negatively affect normal gameplay
- It was created for joke, entertainment, and friendly party use only
- It is not intended for malicious deception, harassment, or ruining other players' experience
- Please inform other players before using it and decide responsibly whether to enable it

### Features

- Automatic activation with no manual use required
- Restarts the countdown after each level initialization
- Shows a clear notice when the deduction cannot be completed

### Best For

- Joke or challenge runs
- Punishment-style gameplay
- Stream interaction or co-op trolling with friends
