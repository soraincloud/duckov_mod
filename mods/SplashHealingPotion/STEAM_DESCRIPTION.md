## English Description

Version: v1.0.1
Updated: 2026-03-18
Update Notes: Halved the potion price, changed each crafting run to produce 2 bottles, and resized the model to 66%.

Adds a new throwable item called Splash Healing Potion. It follows the same throw style as EnderPearl, including an arc preview while aiming. On its first ground impact, the potion bursts at the landing point, restores `50%` of max health to characters within range, and triggers berry-rose splash particles with soft pale highlights together with a landing sound effect.

### Features

- Adds a new throwable AoE healing item: Splash Healing Potion
- Reuses the EnderPearl-style throw arc and aim preview
- Heals characters in range for `50%` of their max health on impact
- Each workbench craft now produces `2` bottles
- Plays landing SFX and spawns berry rose burst particles with soft pale highlights
- The model is now uniformly scaled to `66%` of its original size

### How to Obtain

- Purchase from the Orange NPC
- Price: `$1000`
- Craft at the workbench for `2` bottles per recipe
