版本：v1.0.0

- 当前先复用 EnderPearl 模型资源占位，后续可自行替换

## English Description

Version: v1.0.0
Updated: 2026-03-11
Update Notes: Initial release with a throwable splash healing potion.

Adds a new throwable item called Splash Healing Potion. It uses the same throw feel as EnderPearl, including the grenade arc preview while aiming. On first impact, the potion bursts at the landing point, heals characters in the area for `50%` of their max health, and spawns berry rose splash particles with soft pale highlights.

### Features

- Adds a new throwable AoE healing item: Splash Healing Potion
- Reuses the EnderPearl-style throw arc and aim preview
- Heals characters in range for `50%` of their max health on impact
- Plays landing SFX and spawns berry rose burst particles with soft pale highlights
- Temporarily reuses the EnderPearl model as a placeholder until custom art is provided
