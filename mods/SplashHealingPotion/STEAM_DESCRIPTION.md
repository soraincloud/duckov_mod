# 喷溅治疗药水 | Splash Healing Potion

## 中文介绍

版本：v1.0.2
更新日期：2026-03-18
更新内容：补充命中反馈与音效反馈，治疗药水价格减半，新增两套工作台配方、单次制作改为 2 瓶，并将模型统一调整为 66% 大小。

新增可投掷道具“喷溅治疗药水”。整体手感沿用 EnderPearl 的抛投方式，瞄准时会显示抛物线预览，松手后即可抛出。药水在首次落地碰撞时会当场炸开，为落点周围范围内的角色恢复 `50%` 最大生命值，并触发带发白高光感的莓果玫红爆裂粒子、落地音效，以及命中后的准星治疗反馈；当前也支持两套工作台配方。

### 特色

- 新增可投掷范围治疗道具“喷溅治疗药水”
- 沿用 EnderPearl 式投掷手感与抛物线瞄准
- 落地后对范围内角色恢复 `50%` 最大生命值
- 命中自己时会触发轻量的准星治疗反馈
- 支持两套工作台配方，且每次制作可获得 `2` 瓶
- 落地时触发带发白高光感的莓果玫红爆裂粒子与音效反馈
- 当前模型统一缩放为原始大小的 `66%`

### 获取方式

- NPC 橘子处购买
- 售价：`$1000`
- 工作台配方一：`6x 止血绷带` + `2x 小急救箱` + `1x 恢复针` -> `2` 瓶
- 工作台配方二：`3x 玻璃` + `1x 恢复针` -> `2` 瓶（依赖 `MCPrerequisite` 提供的共享玻璃）

## English Description

Version: v1.0.2
Updated: 2026-03-18
Update Notes: Added hit feedback and sound feedback, halved the potion price, added two workbench recipes that each produce 2 bottles, and resized the model to 66%.

Adds a new throwable item called Splash Healing Potion. It follows the same throw style as EnderPearl, including an arc preview while aiming. On its first ground impact, the potion bursts at the landing point, restores `50%` of max health to characters within range, and triggers berry-rose splash particles with soft pale highlights, landing SFX, and a healing hit indicator around the crosshair. It now also supports two workbench recipes.

### Features

- Adds a new throwable AoE healing item: Splash Healing Potion
- Reuses the EnderPearl-style throw arc and aim preview
- Heals characters in range for `50%` of their max health on impact
- Triggers a lightweight healing hit indicator when you are affected
- Supports two workbench recipes, each producing `2` bottles
- Plays landing SFX and spawns berry rose burst particles with soft pale highlights
- The model is now uniformly scaled to `66%` of its original size

### How to Obtain

- Purchase from the Orange NPC
- Price: `$1000`
- Workbench recipe A: `6x Bandage` + `2x Small First Aid Kit` + `1x Recovery Shot` -> `2` bottles
- Workbench recipe B: `3x Glass` + `1x Recovery Shot` -> `2` bottles
