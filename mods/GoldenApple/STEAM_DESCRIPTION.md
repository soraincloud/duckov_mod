# 附魔金苹果 | Enchanted Golden Apple

## 中文介绍

版本：v1.0.2
更新日期：2026-03-18
更新内容：提高生命上限与持续回血数值，并解决物品丢出来时贴图过小的问题。

为游戏新增可食用物品“附魔金苹果”。食用后会立即获得三段临时增益：生命上限 `+30` 持续 2 分钟，30 秒内每秒回复 `15` 点生命，以及头甲 `+1.5`、身甲 `+1.5` 持续 5 分钟。左下角状态栏会显示三段增益与各自剩余时间，地面掉落时的贴图显示也已放大一倍。

### 特色

- 新增可食用强化道具“附魔金苹果”
- 食用后获得生命上限、持续回血与护甲强化三段增益
- 重复食用会刷新持续时间，不会无限叠层
- 左下角状态栏会分别显示三段增益与剩余时间
- 兼容 MC 前置，在工作台与仓库中进入独立的 `MC` 分类

### 获取方式

- NPC 橘子处购买
- 售价：`$6666`
- 工作台制作：`8x 金锭` + `1x 苹果`

- 需要提高容错率的探索与跑图
- 想兼顾回血、生存和护甲收益的通用玩法

## English Description

Version: v1.0.2
Updated: 2026-03-18
Update Notes: Increased max health and regeneration values, and fixed the dropped world sprite being too small.

Adds a new edible item called the Enchanted Golden Apple. Eating it immediately grants three temporary buffs: `+30` max health for 2 minutes, `15` HP per second for 30 seconds, and `+1.5` head armor plus `+1.5` body armor for 5 minutes. All three effects are shown separately in the lower-left buff bar with their remaining durations, and the dropped world sprite is now twice as large.

### Features

- Adds a new edible power-up item: Enchanted Golden Apple
- Grants temporary max health, regeneration, and armor bonuses after use
- Reusing the item refreshes duration instead of stacking infinitely
- Shows each active effect and its remaining time in the lower-left buff bar
- Compatible with the MC prerequisite and appears under the dedicated `MC` category in workbench and storage UI

### How to Obtain

- Purchase from the Orange NPC
- Price: `$6666`
- Craft at the workbench: `8x Gold Ingot` + `1x Apple`

### Best For

- Temporary pre-fight survivability boosts
- Exploration and movement runs where extra fault tolerance matters
- General-purpose playstyles that want healing, survivability, and armor in one item
