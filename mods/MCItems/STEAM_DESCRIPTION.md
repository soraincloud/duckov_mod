这是一个 MC 物品包，为游戏加入 4 个风格统一、功能完整的 MC 系列物品：

### 包含内容

- `末影珍珠`：投掷后在首次碰撞落点传送使用者
- `附魔金苹果`：食用后获得生命上限、持续回血与护甲增益
- `喷溅治疗药水`：投掷后在范围内恢复目标 50% 最大生命值
- `不死图腾`：放入图腾槽后在致命伤害时自动保命并触发回复效果

### 特性

- 一次加入 4 个 MC 系列功能物品
- 保留各自独立的工作台配方、商店出售与运行时资源

- 同时覆盖位移、强化、治疗、保命四类玩法

## English Description

Version: v1.0.0
Updated: 2026-03-20
Update Notes: Added Ender Pearl, Enchanted Golden Apple, Splash Healing Potion, and Totem of Undying as part of one MC-themed item pack.

This is an MC-themed item pack that adds 4 Minecraft-inspired items to the game:

- Ender Pearl
- Enchanted Golden Apple
- Splash Healing Potion
- Totem of Undying

The mod includes complete shop availability, crafting recipes, icons, sounds, models, and gameplay logic for all four items, making it a good fit for players who want a full set of MC-style utility items in one package.

### Included Items

- `Ender Pearl`: teleports the player to the first impact location after being thrown
- `Enchanted Golden Apple`: grants temporary max health, regeneration, and armor buffs when eaten
- `Splash Healing Potion`: restores 50% max HP in an area when it impacts
- `Totem of Undying`: prevents death from lethal damage when equipped in the totem slot

### Features

- Adds 4 MC-themed functional items in one package
- Preserves separate crafting recipes, merchant entries, and runtime assets for each item
- Remains compatible with shared materials and category tags provided by `MCPrerequisite`
- Covers mobility, buffing, healing, and death protection gameplay in one set

### Availability

- Ender Pearl: sold by Merchant_Equipment and also craftable
- Enchanted Golden Apple: sold by Merchant_Equipment and also craftable
- Splash Healing Potion: sold by Merchant_Equipment and also craftable
- Totem of Undying: sold by Merchant_Equipment and also craftable
